Veeva Seating

Client Furnitubes
Role Designer
Type Product Range

Modular straight and curved seating as part of the mid range products for Furnitubes' catalogue.

Modularity was achieved by 'flexing' the straight geometry of the bench to form curved segments of the same length. These pieces can be bolted together to form perfect circles or bolted to straight pieces to create organic, but geometrically controlled shapes to suit different sites.

A further feature was a straight backrest on the curved benches which offered a cost effective solution. Character was added using tapered steel plates to form the V of the support.

Two distinct looks were developed: Side-to-side and Front-to-back, each with its own merits, in function and form.

Side-to-side
  • Long and chunky slats
  • Linear modularity for long runs
  • Simple, elegant and economical
Front-to-back
  • Short and chunky slats
  • Available as straight and curved
  • Dynamic and versatile, suitable for complex schemes
