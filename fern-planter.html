Industrial Design

FERN Planter

Client Furnitubes
Role Design Project Lead
Year 2021–2022

FERN is a freestanding planter range designed for the outdoor public realm market. The brief was clear: create a range that is easy to manufacture, meets specification requirements for planting depth, and is competitively priced.

As Design Project Lead I coordinated across marketing, sales, design, and production — from initial research and concept workshops through to manufacturing-ready drawings and product launch.

Phase One

Research

The approach was grounded in three research pillars, each informing a different dimension of the product — from how it would sit in the market to how it would be built and used.

Competitor analysis conducted by our marketing team benchmarked pricing and quality, allowing us to identify where Furnitubes sat and where we should aim. Sales discussions and stakeholder workshops surfaced the technical constraints that would shape the range.

1. Market
Competitor analysis across pricing and quality. Metalco and Vestre dominated the high-quality, high-price segment. The opportunity was to move Furnitubes into the high-quality, more competitive price point.
2. User / Client
Discussions with the sales team revealed two distinct buyer profiles — landscape architects and contractors — with different priorities driving their purchasing decisions.
3. Technical
Working closely with production to understand what could be manufactured efficiently, using this to facilitate workshops that balanced market needs and manufacturing constraints.

Phase Two

Concept Development

Understanding the buyer

Discussions with the sales team surfaced two distinct client profiles, each with different priorities. Landscape architects valued variety, flexibility, quality finish, and the ability to contain trees as well as plants. Contractors were focused on price, lead times, ease of installation, and low maintenance.

These insights, alongside the technical workshops, allowed us to take 27 possible planter configurations and refine them down to a core 14 options — covering rectangular, square, and circular forms across two standard heights.

Landscape Architect
  • Variety
  • Tree / plant containment
  • Flexibility
  • Quality finish
Contractor
  • Price
  • Lead times
  • Easy of installation
  • Maintenance

Phase Three

Detailing & Features

Using feedback from workshops and learnings from previous ranges, we developed a set of features that completed the design. These features were then used to build the marketing collateral for the product launch.

Free-standing
Adjustable levelling feet
Durable construction
Fully welded watertight body
Low height — plants
High height — trees
Standard base option
100mm drainage reservoir
Forklift base option
Anchorage for root ball

The result

Taking into account stakeholders' needs, we brought a range of planters to market in March 2022. A back catalogue of feasible sizes and shapes was retained, allowing the range to adapt and scale as user needs evolve over time.
