Public Realm

Kings Crescent

Client MUF Architecture
Location Hackney, London
Role Designer

Working with MUF architects to create feature seating in a new development: Kings Crescent, Hackney. Helping the client with their key objectives and architectural stages as well as project managing through to delivery.

Communication with all stakeholders was essential. Typically 3 different groups: The Architect for design, the main contractor for costs and project delivery, and manufacturing supply chain for constructing the products.

The result was a series of playful interventions in a communal area for the community that encouraged the use of public seating in a more organic and fluid way.
